HTML & CSS reference

Tags, styles and publishing
  • Put visible content in <body>. Give the page an <h1> heading, use <p> for a paragraph, and nest <li> items inside <ul> for a bulleted list.
  • An image uses src for its file and alt for its text alternative. A link uses <a href="…">. A note between <!-- and --> is an HTML comment.
  • Placed in the head, <link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css"> connects pages to shared style rules. color changes the letters. background-color changes the area behind them.
  • A digital footprint can include posts or photos that others save. Use artwork you created or have permission to use, follow its license, ask people before publishing their pictures, and keep personal addresses private.

Move a sprite with a condition

A sprite is an object that can move on a screen. A game can check for key input on each frame. Our grid runner takes one step per keypress. If you start at x = 2, pressing right moves you to x = 3.

if (keyDown("right") && player.x < 4) {
  player.x = player.x + 1;
}

Predict. If the sprite is at x = 4 and you press right, what should happen? Move the runner to the flag, try the edge, then turn the corner. Row 0 is at the top.

Math room

Algebra tiles

Build a rectangle, count its tiles, and give the same area two names. Can you run the pattern backward?

Rectangle lab

A long tile has area x. A small square has area 1. The tiles in one row form a group. Count all the rows to expand, or find equal rows to factor.

Catch the missing term

A stage crew lays out four identical rows. Each row needs 2x + 3 tiles. A crew member writes 4(2x + 3) = 8x + 3. In a group of four rows, where did the other nine small tiles go?

Walk through the correction

Every row gets three small tiles, so four rows need 12. The long tiles give 4 × 2x = 8x. The finished expression is 8x + 12. Reading the rectangle backward shows why 4 is the greatest common factor of 8 and 12.

One mismatch disproves a claim. One match gives you a clue. To explain why two expressions agree for every value, show what happens to both terms when you distribute.

Algebra word bank
Term
A piece of a sum, including its sign. In 5x − 2, the terms are 5x and −2.
Coefficient
The numerical factor multiplying a variable. The coefficient of 5x is 5.
Constant
A fixed value that does not depend on the variable. In 5x − 2, the constant term is −2.
Distribute
Multiply each term inside the parentheses by the factor outside.
Greatest common factor
The largest positive integer that divides each whole-number coefficient and constant without a remainder.

What counts as evidence?

On a page of molecule sketches, two CO₂ molecules are drawn close together. A second drawing shows the same two molecules farther apart. A third drawing shows two CO molecules and one O₂ molecule.

Which drawing shows different substances?

The first and second drawings differ in spacing. Each still contains two CO₂ molecules. In the third drawing the atoms have new partners. Both carbon atoms and all four oxygen atoms are accounted for, and the molecules have changed. This is a comparison of particle diagrams.

Poetry studio

A crooked star. A train heard from an empty platform. Choose a poem and see how much a few words can reveal about its speaker.

Try reading like a poet

Under the pier, the moon sets down
a silver plate on the water.

Notice. The two lines compare moonlight on the water to a silver plate. You can picture its round shape and gleaming surface.

Interpret. The words “sets down” make the moon sound gentle. A reader might imagine the water staying still long enough to hold the reflection. This reading connects a quoted detail to a particular effect.

Choose your evidence
